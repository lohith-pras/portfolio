# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Primarily tech recruiters and hiring managers scanning for 5G/6G, MIMO/ISAC, edge-AI, and embedded/LLM-tooling skills — usually on a quick first pass, deciding in seconds whether to dig deeper. Secondary: research/academic peers wanting depth, and the occasional freelance client. The interface must reward a fast scan first, then hold up to a slow read.

Owner: Lo — Electromobility M.Sc., FAU Erlangen-Nuremberg (AI & Connectivity). Domain: 5G/6G, MIMO, ISAC, cognitive radar, edge AI, LLM tooling. Lived in Bengaluru, Nuremberg, Dresden.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio that proves engineering range through the site itself, not just claims about it. The interactive 3D/motion work (R3F scenes, shader gradient, GSAP transitions) is evidence of craft, not decoration. Success = a recruiter remembers the site, understands the domain fit fast, and reaches the projects + contact path without friction.

## Brand Personality

Technical, bold, playful. Confident engineering voice with quirky interactive touches (chibi character, waving banners, beamforming visuals, places-lived typographic scroll). Racing-inspired energy via the electric-orange/crimson-on-black palette. Specific and concrete, never buzzword-y. Personality woven in subtly (Kannada script, KTM Duke, masala dosa) rather than announced.

## Anti-references

- **Generic SaaS template**: no cream/sand body bg, no tiny uppercase tracked eyebrows on every section, no identical icon-heading-text card grids, no gradient text.
- **Corporate LinkedIn-stiff**: no stock photos, no buzzwords (streamline/leverage/seamless/world-class), no personality-free recital.
- **Overdesigned / unreadable**: effects never cost legibility, contrast, or performance. Motion serves comprehension.
- **Cluttered dev-portfolio**: no wall of skill badges, tech-logo grids, or skill-percentage bars.

## Design Principles

1. **Show, don't tell.** The site demonstrates the skills it claims. Interactive scenes are proof of craft.
2. **Fast scan, slow read.** Reward the 5-second recruiter pass and the deep-dive both; hierarchy carries the scan.
3. **Personality woven, not announced.** Identity details (Kannada, motorsport numbers, hometown food) appear in context, never as callout boxes.
4. **Effect earns its cost.** Every 3D scene, shader, and transition must improve comprehension or memory, never just exist; legibility and performance are non-negotiable.
5. **Concrete over generic.** Specific nouns and real domain terms (MIMO, ISAC, beamforming) over marketing adjectives.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA. Body text ≥4.5:1 against the near-black bg. Every animation (3D scenes, shader, GSAP, banners) needs a `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` alternative — already a stated convention in existing specs; hold all new work to it. Keyboard-navigable interactive controls. Bilingual (en/de) via next-intl.
