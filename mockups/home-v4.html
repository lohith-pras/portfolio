U3 · FAU research project · 2026 →

Radios that adapt to their surroundings

A few months back I started my research project on cognitive ISAC networks, systems that sense and communicate at the same time and adapt to the environment. So far I've built the system model and formulated the problem, deciding the optimisation variables and the constraints. Since the problem is non-convex, we're now moving towards a deep RL agent instead of convex optimisation.
